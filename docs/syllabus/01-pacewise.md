## III. Hard

### A. Advanced PDF Editing & Annotation Engine

- **i. Scope:** Adding text, images, other PDFs, code, markings and shapes to pages; persisting and downloading edited documents.
- **iii. Note:** Rendering, coordinate mapping, page order and export correctness are the key challenges.

### B. Advanced Reading Engine

- **i. Scope:** Normal scroll and book-flip modes, guided/self-paced reading, WPM controls and smooth page navigation.
- **iii. Note:** Complex document layouts, reading synchronization and performance increase its difficulty.

### C. OCR & Document Intelligence

- **i. Scope:** PDF text extraction, scanned documents, handwritten-note understanding and visual understanding integration.
- **iii. Note:** Accuracy for handwriting recognition and poor-quality scans is particularly challenging.

### D. Full-Document Search & Indexing

- **i. Scope:** Finding relevant content from a search query, navigating to the correct page, indexing OCR text and keeping the index up to date after document changes.

### E. Adaptive Learning & Revision Engine

- **i. Scope:** Scheduling based on learning pace, revision of earlier syllabus, weekly tests and performance-based recommendations.
- **ii. Difficulty:** Hard
- **iii. Note:** The real difficulty arises when recommendations are validated against measurable learning improvement.

### F. Multi-User Collaboration & Identity

- **i. Scope:** Multiple sessions, permissions, SSO, shared documents, data isolation and concurrent editing.
- **iii. Note:** SSO integration itself can be comparatively straightforward; reliable permissions and concurrent editing are more difficult.

### G. Content Platform Infrastructure

- **i. Scope:** Drive-like server storage, user-specific documents, feed generation, content deduplication, duplicate-page comparison and copyright-related similarity signals.
- **iii. Note:** Reliable storage, access control and scalable feed/indexing make it complex.
