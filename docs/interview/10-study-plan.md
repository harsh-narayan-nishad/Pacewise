| Week | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | JS/TS internals, event loop, async | 30 JS questions + 5 utility implementations |
| 2 | DSA: arrays, hashing, two pointers, sliding window, stack | 40 problems (15 E / 20 M / 5 H) |
| 3 | DSA: trees, graphs, heaps, binary search | 40 problems |

| 7 | Backend/DB/security, deployment | Write an ADR for this project's deploy |
| 8 | Behavioral stories, mocks, revise weak spots | 8 STAR stories; 3 full mock loops |

## Daily rhythm
60–90 min DSA · 30 min fundamentals · 30 min design reading · 15 min recall of yesterday's mistakes.

## How levels are judged (typical)
- **Easy questions:** must be fast and flawless; hesitation here costs the offer.
- **Medium:** clean solution, correct complexity, tests edge cases unprompted.
- **Hard / design:** structure, trade-offs, failure modes, and clear communication; a partially optimal solution well explained beats a silent perfect one.
- **Senior signal:** you ask clarifying questions, quantify, choose boring technology on purpose, and say what you would monitor.
