# Customer Obsession

*Amazon Leadership Principle 1 of 16.*

## In one line

Start from what the customer needs and work backwards; keep their trust above everything, including your interest in competitors.

## What an interviewer listens for

Do you begin with the customer's problem rather than your solution? Can you show a decision where you gave up something for the customer's long-term trust?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about a time you went beyond the brief for a customer.
- Describe a time customer feedback changed your plan.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Naming the customer vaguely ("users") with no evidence of what they actually said or did.
