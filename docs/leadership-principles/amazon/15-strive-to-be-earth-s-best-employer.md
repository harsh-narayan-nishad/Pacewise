# Strive to be Earth's Best Employer

*Amazon Leadership Principle 15 of 16.*

## In one line

Make the workplace safer, more productive, more diverse and fairer; lead with empathy and care about people's growth.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did you create conditions where others could grow? Did you include voices that were missing?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about a time you improved your team's environment.
- Describe how you supported someone's career.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Treating it as perks and culture events rather than how people are led.
