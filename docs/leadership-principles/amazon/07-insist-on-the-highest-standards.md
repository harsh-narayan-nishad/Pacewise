# Insist on the Highest Standards

*Amazon Leadership Principle 7 of 16.*

## In one line

Keep standards that others may call unreasonable, keep raising them, and fix problems so they stay fixed.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did you fix the root cause? Did you refuse to ship something that was not good enough?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about a time you raised the quality bar.
- Describe a defect you stopped from reaching customers.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Perfectionism with no judgment about what matters, or standards you never actually enforced.
