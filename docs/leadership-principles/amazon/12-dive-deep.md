# Dive Deep

*Amazon Leadership Principle 12 of 16.*

## In one line

Work at every level, know the details, check often, and doubt a story when the numbers disagree with it.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did you go into logs, data or code yourself? Did you notice a metric and an anecdote that did not match?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about a time the data did not match what people believed.
- Describe a problem you traced to its root.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Quoting a dashboard without being able to explain how the number is produced.
