# Hire and Develop the Best

*Amazon Leadership Principle 6 of 16.*

## In one line

Raise the bar with every hire and promotion, spot exceptional people, and take coaching seriously.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did you make others better, not just yourself? Did you hold a high bar when it was inconvenient?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about someone you coached.
- Describe a hiring or promotion decision where you held the bar.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Only describing your own work in a question about developing people.
