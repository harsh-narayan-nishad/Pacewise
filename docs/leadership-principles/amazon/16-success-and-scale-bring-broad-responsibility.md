# Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility

*Amazon Leadership Principle 16 of 16.*

## In one line

As an organisation grows its actions reach further; think about secondary effects on communities and the world and leave things better.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did you consider side effects beyond the immediate goal? Did you give back or leave a system better?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about a decision where you weighed its wider impact.
- Describe a time you left something better than you found it.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Only talking about impact on your own team.
