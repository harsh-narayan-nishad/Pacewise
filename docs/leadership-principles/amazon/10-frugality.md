# Frugality

*Amazon Leadership Principle 10 of 16.*

## In one line

Do more with less; limits breed resourcefulness, and there is no credit for a bigger team or budget.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did a constraint push you to a better idea? Did you avoid spending that did not help the customer?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about a time you delivered with fewer resources.
- Describe a cost you cut without hurting quality.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Cheapness that damages the customer or the team.
