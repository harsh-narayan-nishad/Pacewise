# Earn Trust

*Amazon Leadership Principle 11 of 16.*

## In one line

Listen closely, speak candidly, treat people with respect, be openly self-critical and measure yourself against the best.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did you admit a mistake first? Did you give or take hard feedback well?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about a time you had to rebuild trust.
- Describe a hard conversation you had with a colleague.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

A weakness answer that is secretly a strength.
