# Think Big

*Amazon Leadership Principle 8 of 16.*

## In one line

A small view limits results; set a bold direction that inspires others and look for new ways to serve customers.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did you aim past the obvious next step? Did you bring others along with the vision?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about the boldest idea you proposed.
- Describe how you scaled a small idea.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Big words with no first step or no way to measure success.
