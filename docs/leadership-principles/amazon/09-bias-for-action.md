# Bias for Action

*Amazon Leadership Principle 9 of 16.*

## In one line

Speed matters. Many decisions can be reversed, so do not over-study them; take calculated risks.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did you move with partial data? Did you separate reversible from irreversible decisions?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about a time you acted without all the information.
- Describe a calculated risk you took.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Rushing a decision that could not be undone, or waiting for certainty that never comes.
