# Ownership

*Amazon Leadership Principle 2 of 16.*

## In one line

Act like the owner of the whole outcome, think long term, and never treat a problem as someone else's.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did you act outside your own lane? Did you pick the long-term fix when a quick patch was easier?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about a time you took on something that was not your job.
- Describe a long-term decision that cost you short-term results.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Saying "we" for everything so your own part is invisible, or blaming another team.
