# Learn and Be Curious

*Amazon Leadership Principle 5 of 16.*

## In one line

Never finished learning; curious about new possibilities and willing to act on them.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did curiosity lead to a concrete result? How do you learn something new fast?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about something you taught yourself recently.
- Describe a time curiosity solved a real problem.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Listing courses and certificates with no result they produced.
