# Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit

*Amazon Leadership Principle 13 of 16.*

## In one line

Challenge decisions respectfully even when it is uncomfortable, then commit fully once a decision is made.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did you disagree with evidence, not ego? Once overruled, did you really commit?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager.
- Describe a decision you disagreed with and still carried out.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Disagreeing silently, or committing in words while holding back in practice.
