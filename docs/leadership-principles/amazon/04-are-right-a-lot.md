# Are Right, A Lot

*Amazon Leadership Principle 4 of 16.*

## In one line

Good judgment built on instinct that you actively test: seek other views and try to prove your own belief wrong.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did you look for evidence against your plan? Can you describe a call you got wrong and what you changed?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about a decision you made with incomplete data.
- Describe a time you changed your mind.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Never having been wrong, or only being wrong in a way that makes you look good.
