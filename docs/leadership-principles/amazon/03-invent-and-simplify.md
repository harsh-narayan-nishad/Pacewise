# Invent and Simplify

*Amazon Leadership Principle 3 of 16.*

## In one line

Expect new ideas from your team and from outside it, and keep making things simpler, accepting that new ideas can be misunderstood for a while.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did you remove complexity rather than add features? Did you adopt an idea that was not invented in your team?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about something you simplified.
- Describe an unpopular idea you pushed and how you handled doubt.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

A clever solution that is harder to run than the problem it solved.
