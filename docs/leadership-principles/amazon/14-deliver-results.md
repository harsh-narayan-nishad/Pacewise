# Deliver Results

*Amazon Leadership Principle 14 of 16.*

## In one line

Focus on the inputs that matter, deliver on time with the right quality, and rise above setbacks.

## What an interviewer listens for

Did you deliver despite an obstacle? Can you tie your actions to a measurable result?

## Questions you may be asked

- Tell me about a project that nearly failed and how you finished it.
- Describe your biggest measurable result.

## Build your story (STAR)

1. **Situation**: one or two sentences of context.
2. **Task**: what you were responsible for.
3. **Action**: what *you* did, in the first person.
4. **Result**: a number or a clear outcome, and what you learned.

## Watch out for

Effort and activity described instead of an outcome.
